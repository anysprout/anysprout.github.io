Peace and Conflict Studies Minor – Practicum

Analytical reflection and field-based research developed during my internship with Mutual Aid Disaster Relief (501c3) during the Hurricane Helene response. The paper examines organizational coordination, disaster response logistics, and the role of community-based networks in crisis recovery.

Economics Praxis Project – Free Store Model

Interdisciplinary economics research paper proposing a student-run Free Store at UNCG as a community-engaged learning lab. The paper synthesizes literature on student support, stigma, mutual aid, and civic engagement, then develops an economic model to evaluate institutional viability, student retention, and enrollment yield.
