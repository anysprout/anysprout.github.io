About

My work sits at the intersection of applied economics, systems design, and community-centered development. Before returning to school, I spent years building operational and financial systems for small businesses and mission-driven organizations—often translating complex workflows into tools that creative, non-traditional entrepreneurs could actually use.

That experience shaped my academic focus: using rigorous quantitative methods to evaluate programs and institutions that aim to expand opportunity, strengthen resilience, and reduce barriers to economic mobility. I’m especially interested in how incentive-aligned systems—whether in universities, nonprofits, or digital communities—can create genuine empowerment while also generating sustainable, measurable value.

I’m currently completing an MA in Applied Economics at UNCG, building a portfolio of projects that combine modeling, forecasting, and impact-oriented analysis. This site is a living record of that work, including coursework artifacts, research projects, and applied writing across economics and peace & conflict studies.
