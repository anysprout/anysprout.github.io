Research & Academic Projects

  • Free Store Economic Model (Working Paper) — University of North Carolina Greensboro.
    Developed a marginal cost–benefit framework evaluating the institutional return on investment of a campus-based Free Store. Modeled enrollment yield and retention impacts using institutional revenue data and welfare analysis. Integrates economic theory with incentive-aligned system design for sustainable community outcomes.

  • Steam Platform Time Series Analysis.
    Constructed ARIMA and seasonal forecasting models using panel data of daily player engagement across selected applications. Conducted diagnostic testing, model selection, and out-of-sample forecast evaluation. Applied hierarchical reconciliation methods to examine aggregate and disaggregate engagement patterns within digital platform ecosystems.

  • Story Circle Facilitation in Digital Communities (Praxis Project) — University of North Carolina Greensboro.
    Designed and facilitated an adapted Story Circle process within a livestreamed Twitch community, integrating restorative justice frameworks and elicitive conflict transformation methodology. Evaluated participatory governance, inclusivity practices, and community-building dynamics in digital environments.

  • Practicum Paper – Mutual Aid Disaster Relief (MADR).
    Analyzed decentralized disaster-response logistics during Hurricane Helene response. Examined institutional coordination, resource allocation under uncertainty, and participatory governance within community-based recovery networks.
