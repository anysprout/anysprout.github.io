ECO 664: Time Series and Forecasting

Spring 2025

Coursework

Final Project: View (PDF)  |  Download

Learning Objectives

  • Articulate the forecasting process and express its value to experts and non-experts.
  • Evaluate accuracy of forecasts.
  • Identify and implement benchmark and principal forecasting methods.
  • Communicate forecast results.
  • Develop proficiency in R.

Reflection

ECO 664 provided a comprehensive introduction to the theory and implementation of quantitative forecasting methods. Through weekly quizzes and applied R assignments, I developed practical experience constructing and evaluating forecasts using benchmark methods, regression-based approaches, exponential smoothing models, ARIMA models, dynamic regression models, and hierarchical forecasting techniques. The course emphasized both model construction and diagnostic evaluation, reinforcing the importance of residual analysis, forecast accuracy measures, and appropriate data partitioning.

A central component of the course was the comprehensive final project, which required applying the full forecasting process to a time series of my choice. This project strengthened my ability to move systematically from problem definition and exploratory analysis to model selection, validation, and communication of results. Preparing both a technical written report and a non-technical video presentation required translating quantitative findings into accessible conclusions for different audiences.

More broadly, ECO 664 enhanced my ability to evaluate forecast performance critically and to communicate uncertainty transparently through prediction intervals and accuracy metrics. The emphasis on R throughout the course significantly strengthened my applied programming skills and reinforced the connection between theoretical forecasting concepts and real-world implementation.
