ECO 657: Managerial Economics

Spring 2026

Learning Objectives

By the end of the course, you will be able to:

  1. Setup and solve the fundamental microeconomic models.
  2. Understand the economic intuition underlying these models.
  3. Analyze, interpret and communicate the results that these models produce.
  4. Apply these models to real-world managerial problems.
  5. Use data to inform business decision making.

Reflection

ECO 657 focused on applying microeconomic theory to real-world decision making within firms. The course emphasized translating consumer theory, producer theory, and game theory into practical managerial strategies. Through homework assignments, Excel-based applications, and exams, I strengthened my ability to move from theoretical models to applied business analysis.

The integration of analytical modeling with real-world scenarios reinforced the importance of economic intuition. Rather than stopping at formal solutions, the course required interpreting results in terms of pricing strategy, cost structure, market power, and competitive behavior. This strengthened my ability to communicate economic reasoning in a business context.

By combining theory, quantitative analysis, and application, ECO 657 enhanced my capacity to use economic models as decision-making tools. The course strengthened my ability to analyze strategic interactions, evaluate pricing and production decisions, and incorporate data into managerial problem solving.
