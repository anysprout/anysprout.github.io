ECO 646: Macroeconomics

Fall 2025

Coursework

This project evaluates U.S. monetary policy using multiple rule-based frameworks, including the Taylor rule and alternative parameterizations, applied to historical and projected data. I selected it because it demonstrates empirical estimation, counterfactual simulation, and critical comparison between rule-based prescriptions and observed Federal Reserve behavior.

Portfolio Assignment: View Assignment (PDF)  |  View Write Up (PDF)

Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Identify the key determinants of long-run per capita income and economic growth.
  • Explain the theoretical foundations and empirical implications of modern growth models.
  • Describe the main features of modern business cycles.
  • Apply short-run macroeconomic models to analyze fluctuations in economic activity.
  • Assess how individual behavior and expectations influence the effectiveness of macroeconomic policy.
  • Understand how macroeconomic data is collected, compiled, and interpreted.
  • Identify the major agencies responsible for producing macroeconomic data.

Reflection

ECO 646 gave me a much deeper understanding of national economic policy by making me much more familiar with FRED as a tool and primary source for economic information. Learning how to find relevant series, format the data correctly, apply formulas, and prepare it for analysis made macroeconomic policy feel much more accessible and concrete. Instead of relying only on summaries or interpretation from others, I became more comfortable going to the underlying data myself and evaluating what it suggested.

That experience has had a lasting effect on how I read news reports and press releases about inflation, growth, interest rates, and other major indicators. Spending time working directly with economic data made me more attentive to how claims are framed, what measures are being used, and how different assumptions can shape the story being told. The course strengthened my ability to think critically about macroeconomic policy decisions and gave me a more informed lens for navigating complicated contemporary economic conditions.
