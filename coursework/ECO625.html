ECO 625: Data Methods in Economics

Fall 2024

Coursework

Portfolio Assignment: View (PDF)  |  Download

Learning Objectives

  1. Describe the process of data analysis.
  2. Describe common data issues.
  3. Recall basic syntax of Base SAS data steps and procedures.
  4. Utilize SAS software within SAS Studio to manipulate data.
  5. Produce descriptive statistics and graphs using SAS software.
  6. Analyze data using hypotheses tests and simple and multiple regression using SAS software.

Reflection

ECO 625 served as a foundational course in data handling and empirical workflow within the MA in Applied Economics program. Through structured unit assignments and a cumulative final project, I developed practical experience manipulating datasets, constructing variables, cleaning and combining files, and preparing outputs for research and reporting purposes. The course required consistent application of Base SAS syntax and procedures, reinforcing technical fluency while emphasizing reproducibility and accuracy.

The progression from descriptive statistics and graphing to hypothesis testing and regression modeling strengthened my understanding of how raw data becomes analytical evidence. Rather than treating statistical procedures as abstract formulas, this course grounded them in implementation—requiring me to execute each step in SAS and interpret results carefully. That integration of programming, statistical reasoning, and documentation directly advanced my ability to conduct structured data analysis in professional and research settings.

More broadly, ECO 625 established the technical baseline that supports my later coursework in econometrics, forecasting, and applied policy analysis. By learning to diagnose common data issues, manage missing values, and structure analytical workflows properly, I developed habits that carry forward into more advanced modeling contexts. This course therefore contributed significantly to my development as an applied economist by strengthening both my technical execution and my understanding of the full data analysis process.
