ECO 630: Introduction to Cost-Effectiveness

Spring 2026

Coursework

Final Project: View (PDF)  |  Download

Learning Objectives

  • Provide an introduction to the methods used by health economists to evaluate health care technologies and programs.
  • Learn how to critique and use health economic evaluations conducted by others.
  • Use some common health economic evaluation tools and methods.

Reflection

ECO 630 introduced the formal tools and frameworks used in health economic evaluation, with a particular emphasis on cost-effectiveness analysis. Through structured assignments and a cumulative final project, I developed the ability to assess cost data, measure health outcomes, construct incremental cost-effectiveness ratios, and conduct sensitivity analyses to evaluate uncertainty.

The course emphasized both methodological rigor and critical evaluation. Rather than simply applying formulas, I was required to examine underlying assumptions, identify potential biases in outcome measurement, assess the appropriateness of discounting and perspective, and evaluate whether reported results were policy-relevant. This strengthened my ability to critique published economic evaluations and interpret findings responsibly.

By integrating theoretical foundations from health economics with practical modeling exercises, ECO 630 enhanced my ability to apply economic evaluation methods in real-world health and policy contexts. The course significantly advanced my capacity to analyze tradeoffs in resource allocation and to communicate cost-effectiveness findings clearly and rigorously.
